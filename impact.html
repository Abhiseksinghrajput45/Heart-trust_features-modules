What we measure

  • Healthcare: people reached, camps held, referrals completed.

  • Biomass recycling: tonnes collected or recycled, where measured.
  • Education: schools engaged, students trained.
  • Women’s empowerment: women trained, SHGs engaged.
  • Welfare support: applications assisted, referrals completed.
  • Community engagement: villages reached, active volunteers.

Only measured and verified figures are published, with reporting periods and clear definitions.
