07 — FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Are the health camps free?

Yes. Camps are free for villagers. Sponsors and donors fund the medicines, doctors and logistics.

Who sees my health records?

Only authorised healthcare professionals, and only with your consent. Access is role-based and logged.

Do you replace government schemes?

No. We explain eligibility and direct you to the official channel. Approvals are decided by the department.

How can I help?

Volunteer, sponsor a camp or village, or partner with us as a doctor, company or university.
