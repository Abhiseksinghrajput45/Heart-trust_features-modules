Nutrition & Family Wellness

Patient data is shared only with consent and role-based access.

What our health programme covers

  • Upcoming health camp calendar and village schedules.
  • Doctor consultations, BP and diabetes screening.
  • Eye and dental health awareness.
  • Patient referral and follow-up coordination.
  • Mobile medical outreach programme.
  • Volunteer and healthcare professional registration.

Nutrition & family wellness

  • Maternal and child nutrition awareness.
  • Anaemia and malnutrition prevention education.
  • Healthy recipes using locally available foods.
  • Breastfeeding and infant feeding awareness.
  • Adolescent and menstrual health education.
  • Elderly care and healthy lifestyle resources.

Digital health services (rolling out in phases)

  • Online health camp registration and appointment requests.
  • Telemedicine consultation requests and follow-up scheduling.
  • Telepathology and laboratory referral coordination.
  • Health education articles in Odia and relevant local languages.
  • Patient follow-up reminders and referral status.
  • Clinic and healthcare service directory.
  • Consent-based patient records for authorised healthcare professionals.

Clinical services are delivered by qualified professionals. Patient information is protected through consent, access controls and applicable data protection requirements.
