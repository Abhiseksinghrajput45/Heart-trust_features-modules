About HEART Trust

Mission

An integrated community development organisation supporting tribal communities in Nabarangpur, Odisha through healthcare, nutrition, agriculture, education, digital inclusion and sustainable livelihoods.

HEART coordinates services across domains and links to government platforms rather than duplicating them.

Vision

Healthy, empowered and climate-resilient tribal communities.
